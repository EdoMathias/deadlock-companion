## Ultimate Ready Alerts

Never miss an ultimate again. **Ultimate Ready Alerts** is a new in-game overlay that pops a compact notification whenever a hero unlocks or recharges their ultimate ability — across all players in the match.

### What You Can Do

- **Track every ult unlock** – get notified the moment a hero spends their first skill point to unlock their ultimate ability.
- **Know when every ult is back** – receive a notification each time a hero's ultimate comes off cooldown and is ready to cast again.
- **Filter by team** – choose to receive alerts for Enemies, Allies, and yourself independently, all toggled from the Overlay Editor.
- **See who it is at a glance** – each card shows the hero portrait, name, a clear status label (Ult Unlocked / Ult Ready), and a small relation tag (Enemy / Ally / You).
- **Spot friend from foe instantly** – cards are color-coded by relation (red for enemies, green for your team). Toggle the colored border, background tint, and appear-pulse independently in the Overlay Editor.
- **Position it where you want** – dock the overlay to any screen position through the Overlay Editor.
- **Control how long it stays** – set the auto-dismiss timeout from 5 to 30 seconds to match your play style.
