## Hero Stats - Know the Meta

Stay ahead of the curve with **Hero Stats**, a new view that surfaces win rates, pick rates, and ban rates for every hero — filtered to match your playstyle and rank.

### What You Can Do

- **Analyze win rates** – see at a glance which heroes are dominating the current meta.
- **Track pick and ban rates** – understand which heroes your opponents gravitate toward.
- **Filter by game mode** – separate Normal and Street Brawl results to compare like for like.
- **Narrow by rank** – focus on data from your skill tier to see what's actually winning in your bracket.
- **Set a date range** – zoom into any patch window to track how the meta has shifted over time.
- **Exclude first-time tryouts** – set a minimum match count to see how heroes perform in experienced hands.
- **Sort any column** – click a column header to rank heroes by win rate, pick rate, ban rate, or total matches.

*Note: Hero Stats are powered by a community-driven open-source API. Coverage improves as more players contribute matches to the ecosystem.*

## Live Match - Enemy Item Tracking

Following recent changes to **Overwolf's Game Events**, damage scores for enemy players are no longer available. Your scoreboard now shows what the enemy team is building instead — keeping the view useful and giving you something meaningful to act on.

### What You Can Do

- **Track enemy item builds** – each enemy player's current items are displayed directly in the scoreboard during a live match.
- **Identify threats at a glance** – hover over any item icon to see its name instantly.
