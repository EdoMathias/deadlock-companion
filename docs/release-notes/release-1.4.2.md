## Ranked, Everywhere - Plus Filters That Remember You

A cleanup release: the Ranked queue now shows up throughout the app, and your filter settings finally stay where you left them.

### Ranked matches are labelled at a glance

The gold **Ranked** badge used to appear only after you opened a match. Now it shows:

- **In the match list** - spot your ranked games without clicking into a single one.
- **In Live Match** - the header calls out a ranked game the moment you're in one.
- **Right after a match ends** - a game you just finished is tagged Ranked immediately, before it reaches the community database.

### Ranked stats in every mode dropdown

**Hero Stats**, **Item Stats**, and **Match History** all gained a **Ranked** option. The mode picker now reads:

- **Normal + Ranked** - the whole standard queue (what the app has always shown, and still the default)
- **Normal** - unranked games only
- **Ranked** - ranked games only
- **Street Brawl**

So you can finally answer "is this hero actually good in ranked?" without leaving the Standard table. Win rates, pick rates, ban rates, and item stats all respect the selection.

The mode dropdowns also got a visual refresh, matching the hero picker and rank slider next to them.

### Your filters stay put

Set up **Hero Stats** the way you like it - mode, patch range, rank range, minimum matches, sorting, even the Ranked vs Normal tab - navigate away, and come back to find it exactly as you left it. It survives restarting the app, too. **Item Stats** and **Match History** remember their filters as well.

New **Reset** button in the Hero Stats and Item Stats filter bars puts everything back to defaults whenever you want a clean slate.
