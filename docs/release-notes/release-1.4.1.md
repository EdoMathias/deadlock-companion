## Ranked Update - Refreshed Ranks & a New Win-Rate View

Deadlock's Matchmaking Update reworked ranks, and Deadlock Companion now keeps up.

### Updated rank names & badges

The rank tiers have new names and freshly redrawn badges. Everywhere you filter by rank - in **Hero Stats** and **Item Stats** - you'll see the current art and the correct names (Acolyte, Sentinel, Mystic, Ritualist, Emissary, and the rest), matching what you see in game.

### "Matchmaking Update" added to the patch selector

The patch dropdown now includes the **Matchmaking Update (2026-07-30)** as the latest entry, so you can filter stats to just the current ranked meta.

### New: Ranked vs Normal hero win rates

Ever wondered which heroes are stronger in ranked than in normal play? In **Hero Stats**, switch to the new **Ranked vs Normal** tab to see each hero's ranked win rate, normal win rate, and the difference (Δ) side by side. Sort by the delta to instantly spot who over- or under-performs once the games get serious.

## Privacy & Analytics - Transparency First

We've added **anonymous usage analytics** to help us understand which features are genuinely useful and where the app can improve. We built this privacy-first, and you're always in control.

### What This Means

- **No personal information, ever** - we do not collect your Steam ID, your player name, or any personally identifiable data.
- **Anonymous only** - usage is tied to a random, per-install identifier - never to you.
- **What we look at** - which screens and features get used, and whether core features (like the in-game overlays) are working correctly. That's it.
- **Opt out anytime** - open **Settings → Privacy** and turn off *"Share anonymous usage analytics"* whenever you like.

We added this so future updates are driven by how the app is really used - not guesswork.
