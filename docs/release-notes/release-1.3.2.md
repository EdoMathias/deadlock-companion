## Counter Items - Build to Counter

Walk into every fight with a plan. **Counter Items** is a new in-game overlay that recommends the best items to buy against the specific enemies you're facing — surfaced live during the match and refreshed as the game evolves.

### What You Can Do

- **See what counters your lobby** – item recommendations are pulled from real match data, filtered by your hero versus the enemy team's heroes.
- **Toggle individual enemies** – click any enemy portrait to include or exclude them, so you can focus on the threat you actually need to answer.
- **Stay time-relevant** – the list trims to items players typically buy around your current match time, so you're not staring at late-game options in the laning phase.
- **Refresh on your terms** – choose how often the list re-evaluates against match time in the Overlay Editor, anywhere from 1 to 10 minutes.
- **Position it where you want** – dock the overlay to any of eight screen positions through the Overlay Editor.
- **Show or hide it instantly** – press Alt+Shift+F to toggle the overlay any time during a match.

*Note: Recommendations are powered by an open-source community-driven API. Coverage and accuracy improve as more players contribute matches.*
