Welcome to the first release of **Deadlock Companion** - your in-game and desktop companion for tracking matches, analyzing performance, and contributing to a growing community-driven data ecosystem.

---

## Core Features

### Live Match
- Automatically detects when a match is running.
- Displays a **live scoreboard overlay** during gameplay.
- Get a quick glance at match stats without leaving the game.

---

### Match History
- View your **recent matches** in a clean, structured list.
- Each match includes:
  - **Summary Tab** – powered by Overwolf Game Events.
  - **Detailed Tab** – deeper stats powered by an open-source API.
- Note: Detailed stats may not be immediately available.

---

### Contribute (Community-Driven Data)
- Help power the ecosystem by uploading your **Steam Cache folder**.
- The app extracts required match IDs and submits them to the API.
- Once processed, this enables **Detailed stats** for your matches.
- Processing may take some time depending on the API.

---

### Profile
- View your **personal stats and performance metrics**.
- Track your overall progress and gameplay trends.

---

## Community-Driven Platform

Deadlock Companion is built on an **open-source, open-data API**.

- Many features rely on **community contributions**.
- The more players contribute data, the richer and more accurate the stats become.
- Be part of building the ultimate Deadlock data hub.

---

## Known Issues

- Due to recent changes in **Overwolf's Game Events**, some **team stats may appear as 0**.
- We are actively working on a solution.
- For now, the feature remains available to avoid major disruptions.
