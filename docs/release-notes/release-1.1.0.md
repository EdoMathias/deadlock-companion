## Item Tier List Update

---

The new **Item Tier List** view gives you deeper insight into item performance so you can make better decisions during your matches.

### What You Can Do

- **Analyze win rates** – See how each item performs across matches.
- **Compare costs** – Evaluate value versus effectiveness at a glance.
- **Track timing** – Understand when top players typically purchase items.
- **Make informed builds** – Use real data to refine your strategy.

---

## Item Purchase Alerts - Real-Time Awareness

Stay one step ahead with **Item Purchase Alerts**, powered by Overwolf game events.

### What You Can Do

- **Select items to watch** – Choose which items you care about through the **Item Stats View**.
- **Get instant notifications** – Know when enemies purchase tracked items.
- **Adapt faster** – React in real time to enemy power spikes.

---

## Overlay Editor - Customize Your Experience

Take control of your in-game interface with the new **Overlay Editor**.

### What You Can Do

- **Move overlay windows** – Position alerts exactly where you want them.
- **Toggle item descriptions** – Show or hide additional item details.
- **Adjust notification timing** – Control how long alerts stay visible.

*Note: Currently supports Item Purchase Alerts only.*
